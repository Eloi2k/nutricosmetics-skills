# Example: problem-to-product, menopause

Fictional brand. Shortened run to show the format. Evidence summaries reflect the cited sources at the time of writing; re-check before real use. Not medical advice.

## Stage 1: Problem map

Target: perimenopausal and early postmenopausal women, 45-58, active, who do not use or do not want hormone therapy.

| Symptom / goal | Priority | Customer words (typical) | Frustration with current options |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hot flashes, night sweats | High | "waking up drenched", "flushes in meetings" | Supplements that "did nothing", fear of hormones |
| Sleep | High | "awake at 3am every night" | Sleep pills feel heavy |
| Mood, irritability | Medium | "short fuse", "not myself" | Unsure what is hormonal |
| Bone health (long term) | Medium | "my mum had osteoporosis" | Too many pills |

Disease line: osteoporosis, clinical depression and insomnia disorder are out of scope; mild, common life-stage symptoms are in scope.

Job to be done: "Get through the menopause transition feeling like myself, without hormones and without a cabinet of pills."

## Stage 2: Ingredient evidence (extract)

| Ingredient | Outcome | Grade | Studied dose | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soy isoflavones | Hot flash frequency | A/B | Median ~54 mg/day (aglycone), 6 weeks to 12 months | Meta-analysis of RCTs: ~20% fewer hot flashes vs placebo. Medical advice for women with a history of hormone-sensitive cancer. No authorized EU claim. |
| Black cohosh (isopropanolic extract) | Hot flashes | B/C | Per specific extract | Results vary by extract; some reviews find no effect. Historical liver-safety concerns, later reviews reassuring; keep a warning. |
| Vitamin D + calcium | Bone maintenance | A (authorized nutrients) | Per NRV | EU/CH authorized claims for normal bones. |
| Vitamin B6 | Hormonal activity | Authorized nutrient claim | ≥15% NRV | EU/CH claim: contributes to the regulation of hormonal activity. |
| Magnesium | Tiredness | Authorized nutrient claim | ≥15% NRV | EU/CH claim: contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue. |

## Stage 3: Formula brief

Capsule, 2 per day: soy isoflavones at the studied dose (hero), vitamin D, vitamin B6, magnesium (supporting, authorized claims). Calcium left out: the dose does not fit in 2 capsules. Cure: 12 weeks minimum, matching the trials. Warnings: consult a doctor if history of hormone-sensitive cancer, during treatment with tamoxifen or thyroid medication.

## Stage 4: Claims per market

| Market | What the product page can say |
| --- | --- |
| US | Structure/function wording about common, mild symptoms of menopause with substantiation and the FDA disclaimer, e.g. "Supports women through menopause and helps with occasional hot flashes.*" Avoid "stops" or "treats". |
| EU / UK / CH | No claim on isoflavones. "Vitamin B6 contributes to the regulation of hormonal activity." "Vitamin D contributes to the maintenance of normal bones." "Magnesium contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue." Isoflavones named with dose and source only. |

## Stage 5: Concept and test

Positioning: for women in the menopause transition who want a single, hormone-free daily routine, built on the ingredient with the most human evidence for hot flashes, at the dose used in studies.

Validation: waitlist landing page with two angles ("one capsule routine" vs "the dose studies used"), 10 interviews from the waitlist, success threshold set before launch.
