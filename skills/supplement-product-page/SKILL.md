# Supplement product page

A supplement page has to do two jobs at once: convince a skeptical buyer to start a cure, and stay inside food-supplement law. Write for both.

## Inputs to collect

Ask only for what is missing:
- Product name, format (powder, capsules, shots), pack size, days per pack, price and any subscription price.
- Full ingredient list with amount per daily dose (needed to know which nutrients can carry an authorized claim).
- Target customer and the main problem they want to solve, in their words (reviews, DMs, support tickets).
- Proof available: studies on the finished product or ingredients (dose, duration, population), certifications, origin, manufacturing.
- Market(s): Switzerland, EU, other.

Never invent studies, percentages, review counts or certifications. If proof is missing, write the copy without the claim and list what proof would unlock it.

## Page structure (top to bottom)

1. **Above the fold:** product name, one-line promise built on an authorized claim or a sensory/lifestyle benefit, price per day (not only per pack), primary CTA, subscription toggle if offered, 2-3 trust markers that are true (origin, number of reviews, guarantee).
2. **Who it is for / when to take it:** the moment of life and the desired outcome, without naming a disease or skin condition as something the product treats.
3. **What's inside and why:** each key ingredient with dose and its role. Attach health claims only to nutrients that carry an authorized claim; describe other ingredients factually (source, form, dose).
4. **The cure:** how to take it, how long a cure lasts, what to expect and when, phrased as experience and habit, not guaranteed results.
5. **Proof:** study summary with real numbers and source, reviews with verified-buyer marks, press or expert endorsements only if real and current.
6. **Offer and risk reversal:** pack options with price per day, subscription savings and flexibility (skip, pause, cancel), guarantee terms if any.
7. **FAQ:** taste/texture, when to see effects, combining with other supplements, pregnancy/breastfeeding and medication (refer to a doctor), allergens, origin of collagen (fish/bovine), shipping.
8. **Mandatory mentions:** "food supplement", recommended daily dose, do not exceed it, not a substitute for a varied diet and healthy lifestyle, keep out of reach of young children, plus allergens.

## Conversion checks

- Price per day visible near the CTA; cure length matches pack size.
- One primary CTA repeated after proof and after FAQ.
- Mobile first: promise, price and CTA visible without scrolling.
- Review widget present and showing real reviews; remove any placeholder counters.
- Subscription framed as convenience (never run out, cure continuity) before discount.

## Output

Return the full page copy by section, then a checklist of: claims to verify (run the claims-check skill if available), proof the user must supply, and mandatory mentions present or missing.
