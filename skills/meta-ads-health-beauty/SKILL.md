# Meta ads for health and beauty supplements

Supplement ads fail in two ways: they get rejected (or the account gets restricted), or they get approved but sound like every other "glow from within" ad. Solve both.

## Policy guardrails (check the current Meta Advertising Standards before launch)

- **Personal attributes:** do not assert or imply the viewer has a condition or physical trait ("Your wrinkles...", "Tired of your cellulite?", "Struggling with acne?"). Speak about the product, the routine or a third person's experience instead.
- **Negative self-perception:** avoid copy or images that make people feel bad about their body to sell the solution.
- **Before/after:** treat before/after images as high risk for health and appearance products; prefer routine, texture, lifestyle and product shots.
- **Health claims:** Meta approval does not make a claim legal; copy must also pass Swiss/EU food-supplement rules (use claims-check).
- **Weight/body products** (e.g. cellulite, contouring): extra restrictions and age targeting rules may apply; check before launch.

## Angles that work without breaking rules

- **Ritual and habit:** the 30-second morning moment, taste, texture, how it fits a routine.
- **Ingredient credibility:** origin, dose, form, Swiss quality and production, tested formula (only real facts).
- **Founder or expert voice:** why the formula exists, what it contains, what it does not.
- **Social proof:** real reviews (experience wording, not medical outcomes), number of customers if true.
- **Comparison of formats:** powder vs gummies vs creams, cost per day vs a skincare routine.
- **Gift and self-care moments:** seasonal, events, cure as a reset.

## Creative system

- Write 3-5 hooks per angle (first 3 seconds of video or first line of text); test angles before polishing executions.
- Formats: UGC talking head, product demo (preparation, texture), founder video, static with review quote, carousel of ingredients.
- Each test states the hypothesis, the metric (hook rate, CTR, CPA) and the expected direction.

## Account structure (starting point)

- Prospecting: broad or Advantage+ audience with several creatives per ad set; let creative do the targeting.
- Retargeting and existing customers: separate budget or exclusions so prospecting results stay readable.
- Track purchases with pixel plus Conversions API; judge on CPA/ROAS over a full cure cycle, since first-order ROAS undervalues a repeat product.

## Output

Return angles with 3-5 hooks each, full ad copy variants (primary text, headline, description), a policy-risk note per ad (low/medium/high and why), and a short test plan.
